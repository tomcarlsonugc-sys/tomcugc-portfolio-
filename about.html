About

An engineer turned creator who solves every brief for one thing: the sale.

Based in Los Angeles, I'm a fitness fanatic and tech junkie who spent years as an engineer. I approach every brand the way I approached engineering — find the real problem, then solve it.

For UGC, that means understanding exactly what your product does for the customer: opening on a hook that earns the next two seconds, and closing on a CTA that makes people want to buy right now.

I've made content for 100+ brands across health, wellness and fitness, tech and wearables, and apparel — from supplement reviews to product demos to grooming routines — always shot to look native to the platform it runs on.
