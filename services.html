FAQ

Common questions.

What is UGC (user-generated content)?

UGC is authentic, creator-shot video or photo content made to look native to social platforms like TikTok, Instagram and YouTube — as opposed to polished studio ads. Brands use it for organic posting, paid ad campaigns, or both.

Do you offer usage rights or whitelisting for paid ads?

Yes — usage rights and whitelisting for paid social campaigns can be added to any package. Let me know your ad platform and flight length when you reach out.

What industries do you create UGC for?

Primarily health, wellness, fitness, sports and tech brands, with 100+ brands served including True Classic, C4 Energy, Harry's, Joymode and HackMotion — see the full portfolio by category.

How does the process work?

Send your product and goal, I come back with hook and script concepts, we align on direction, then I shoot, edit and deliver — with a CTA built to convert.
